INTRODUCTION

About Me

I am Dr. U. Saravanakumar, Professor and Head of the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Muthayammal Engineering College (Autonomous), Tamil Nadu, India.

My academic and research interests include VLSI Design and Antenna Engineering, with a focus on advancing research, supporting student development and strengthening industry-oriented engineering education.

I have more than 70 research publications and have authored three books published by IGI Global, Springer and CRC Press. I am involved in doctoral research supervision and academic editorial activities.
